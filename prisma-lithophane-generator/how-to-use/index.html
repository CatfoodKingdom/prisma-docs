How To Use

Jul 27, 20263 min read

A color lithophane is a thin printed panel that reveals a color image when lit from behind. You give Prisma a photo and the filaments available to you; it works out a printable stack and exports the files your slicer needs.

This section is the practical guide to the app. It explains what to do and what each choice changes. For a conceptual view of the machinery underneath, see How It Works.

The workflow in the app

The conceptual workflow and the controls are nearly the same, but a few decisions live outside the numbered tabs:

In the appWhat you are deciding
Configuration in the left railWhich calibrated filaments and printer setup are available. This is mostly one-time setup.
1 ImageWhich image area to print, its physical size, and any image adjustments.
2 PaletteWhich colored filaments the solve may use.
Settings gearOptional solve, preprocessing, region, and white-cap behavior for the next run.
Solve → 3 PreviewBuild a run, inspect the prediction, and compare it with earlier runs.
4 ExportChoose a completed run and generate its 3MF or STL files and any Swap Instructions.
3D PrintingMove the generated files into the slicer, prepare the printer, and light the finished panel.

Most iteration happens between Palette, Settings, and Preview. Change one meaningful thing, solve again, and compare the runs side by side.

New here? Getting Started covers installing and launching the app and walks through a first solve.

Appearance

Use the Theme control in the right side of the top bar, beside Clear Temp Files, to choose System, Light, or Dark. System follows your operating-system appearance, including changes made while Generator is open; an explicit Light or Dark choice stays fixed. Generator saves the preference in this browser and synchronizes it between open Generator tabs. It is an interface preference only—not part of Settings Profiles, saved runs, or exports.

Setup

  • Configuration — tell Prisma which filaments you own and which printer setup is active.

Make a lithophane

  • Image — choose a photo, set the physical print size, and frame it.
  • Palette — choose colored filaments manually or ask Prisma to suggest them.
  • Settings — tune print thickness, solve behavior, preprocessing, and the white cap when needed.
  • Preview — build and compare predicted results.
  • Improving a Solve — compare palettes first, then use targeted settings changes for specific visible defects.
  • Export — generate print files for a selected completed run.

Special workflow

  • Runs with Swaps — use more colored filaments than the printer can hold at once and carry the resulting Swap Instructions into the slicer.

After export

  • 3D Printing — slice, print, and light the result.
  • Tutorials — worked examples from start to finish.

Clear Temp Files in the top bar removes accumulated working files, solve and export history, automatic saves, temporary saved-run sources, and internal caches—including prepared copies of HEIC, HEIF, and AVIF sources. It never deletes originals in Generator/Images, exported files, or runs you explicitly saved. If a temporary saved-run source was active, load that saved run again to restore it. Prepared conversion copies are rebuilt when an image is next needed or the library is refreshed.
