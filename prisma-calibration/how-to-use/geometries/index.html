Geometries

Jul 28, 20262 min read

The Geometries tab contains reusable calibration-strip designs. A geometry defines the strip’s swatches, material roles, and thicknesses. It does not specify which filaments fill those roles. Filaments are assigned when you create a sample in the Sample Logbook.

📸 Screenshot to capture

The Geometries tab.

Understand the role stack

Each material role becomes a separate body in the exported model. Roles are labeled from the bottom of the printed stack upward: LR_01 is the bottom role, LR_02 is above it, and so on.

A geometry has exactly one Variable role. Its thickness changes from swatch to swatch so one strip measures several thicknesses. Every Fixed role keeps one thickness across the strip. Fixed describes thickness, not purpose: a fixed role may sit above or below the variable role and may contain any filament.

The builder displays the physical stack from top to bottom. Moving a role changes its position and updates its LR label. For a fuller explanation, see Sample Geometry.

Read the library

Each row shows the strip diagram, Alias, Bundle, # Layers (the number of material roles), Used by, and Last modified. Click a row to inspect its ID, bundle membership, exports, and samples.

What you do here

  • + New Sample Geometry — design a new sample geometry. See New Sample Geometry.
  • Click a row — inspect its details, export it, rename it, or change its bundle membership. See Geometry Details.
  • Manage Bundles — map roles across several geometries for coordinated sample creation. See Manage Bundles.
  • Export Geometry Files — export STEP or STL files for geometries used by samples or for the whole library.

Geometry files

The geometry definition is the permanent record. Printable STEP and STL files are generated from it and may not exist until you export them. See Export Sample Geometry for the required role-to-filament matching.

Export Geometry Files opens a batch workflow. Missing Only creates absent files. Force Export can replace existing exports after confirmation. You may export Only Geometries Used By Samples or All Geometries, in STEP, STL, or both formats.
