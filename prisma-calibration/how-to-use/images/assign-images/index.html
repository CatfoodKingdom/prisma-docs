Assign Images

Jul 15, 20265 min read

Before the app can read colors from a sample, it needs two images: the sample image (the printed strip on the lightbox) and a blank image (the same lit lightbox with nothing on it). The blank image lets the app correct for uneven illumination.

For that to work, the app aligns the blank image with the sample image point by point — so the camera must not move, and the lighting must not change, between the blank and the samples in that session.

Capture one consistent session

  • Fix the camera and lightbox in place before capturing the blank image. If either moves, begin a new session with a new blank.
  • Keep exposure, focus, camera orientation, and lighting unchanged throughout the session. Disable automatic changes where your camera workflow allows it.
  • Let the lightbox reach a stable state before capturing the blank and samples.
  • Keep the complete strip and enough surrounding lightbox visible for extraction; do not clip the strip at the frame edge.
  • Preserve a reliable association between each image and its physical sample ID until assignment is complete.

The current Image Inbox imports .cr2, .dng, .tif, and .tiff files. Put supported files in Calibration\Inbox before using Import from Inbox. If you are unsure which installation is active, Open Inbox Folder opens the exact folder used by this Calibration app.

You capture everything on the lightbox outside the app; this screen connects those images to your samples. Open the Images tab and stay on the Assign Images subtab. It has three panes, left to right.

Image Inbox (center)

Where images enter the app. Put supported files in Calibration\Inbox, then click Import from Inbox. Open Inbox Folder opens that location in File Explorer. The top-bar Refresh Data action only refreshes the displayed state; it does not import waiting files. Nothing on this screen can use an image until you import it.

Imported images are grouped into Unassigned, Assigned, and Ignored. Each usable image has a rotate button and an × button to ignore it. Rotating an assigned image clears that sample’s open-side direction so it can be set again. Once a sample is measured, its image cannot be rotated until the result is rejected or reset.

Click a photo to select it. Below the grid, Selected Image names your pick, with two actions:

  • ← Register as Blank turns the selected image into a lightbox reference (see the left pane).
  • Assign Image to Sample → attaches it to the selected sample as its sample image.

Registered Blanks (left)

Your list of registered blank images. Add one by selecting an image and clicking ← Register as Blank, or by dragging an image onto this pane.

Use one blank for every sample captured in the same unchanged session. Click a blank, then Assign Blank to Sample → to attach it to the selected sample. Click the small × on a blank card to unregister it.

Assign Images and Blanks (right)

Samples are split into Ready For Processing and Needs Assignment. Click a sample to make it the target for the → buttons in the other panes. Each card shows its strip diagram and filament list, plus an image slot and a blank slot. Click the small × on either slot to remove that assignment.

Every card also has a four-arrow control for the strip’s open-side direction. Choose the arrow that points toward the open side in the sample image so the app reads the swatches in the correct order. A sample is not ready until it has a sample image, blank image, and open-side direction. Ready samples show a ready badge.

You can also drag an image from the inbox directly onto a sample card’s empty image or blank slot.

One sample card showing its image slot, blank slot, open-side direction control, and ready badge.

Before processing

For every sample, confirm that the physical strip, sample record, sample image, blank image, and open-side direction all agree. When each sample shows ready, continue to Processing Queue.

Doing more at once

Prisma keeps managed copies of imported images. Two Image Inbox actions operate on that managed set:

  • Clean Up Unused removes imported images that no sample uses from active custody and moves their source files to Removed Images inside the inbox.
  • CSV Bulk Assignment assigns sample images, blank images, and orientations to many samples at once. See CSV Assignment Import.

To see how the blank is actually used when the app reads a strip, see Extraction.
