How It Works

Jul 28, 20262 min read

The calibration app measures how filaments transmit light and fits the color models the generator prints from. The workflow: create samples (a strip geometry plus filaments), assign each sample its raw lightbox photographs, extract per-swatch color measurements, and fit models.

This section explains the phenomena — measurement, the models, their corrections. How To Use covers driving the workflow.

The workflow

Dashed items sit outside the app: sample geometries are 3D-printed as physical calibration samples, then photographed on a lightbox as controlled raw photos. Generator receives the filament list and all three models only when you explicitly publish them together as an immutable library.
